BIRTHDAY WEBSITE - QUICK START
1. Open config.js in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code). Change her name, your name,
   the start date, the messages and photo captions.
2. Copy your photos into images/ and your songs into songs/ (names must match config.js).
3. Double-click index.html to test it on your computer (needs internet once for the fonts).
4. To share it, upload the WHOLE folder to a free host: Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop),
   GitHub Pages, Vercel or Cloudflare Pages. Send her the link.
